# Arbetsförmedlingen — Support and programmes A–Z

**Official (English):** [Support and programmes A–Z](https://arbetsformedlingen.se/other-languages/english-engelska/additional-support/support-a-z)

Catalogue of **extra support and programmes** for people registered as jobseekers (or with special needs). Eligibility is always an **individual AF assessment** — this note is a map, not a claim of entitlement.

## Why keep this

- AF runway / benefits reporting pairs with Platsbanken hunt ([[Projects/collab-finder/README|collab-finder]] **Sweden** screen).
- English index of programmes when Swedish forms are slow to parse.
- Quick scan before caseworker meetings: what exists, what to ask about.
