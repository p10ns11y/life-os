# Career breaks in Sweden — types, law, how firms actually treat them

Not legal advice. Statutes change; Försäkringskassan / a-kassa / DO decide cases. Operator frame (what *you* did, letter line): `private/career/` only.

**Spelling:** English **sabbatical**. Swedish **sabbatsår** / **karriärpaus** / **lucka**.

## Glossary (Swedish → English)

Use the English column in English-language ATS. Keep the Swedish word in parentheses once if the reader is Swedish HR.

| **provanställning** | **probation** (often 6 months; can end before it becomes permanent) | |

| Arbetsförmedlingen (AF) | **Public Employment Service** | |

| tillsvidare vs lucka | You want a **permanent IC role**; the unpaid interval is an **employment gap** you label, not leave of absence | |

Two layers always:

1. **Law** — what you may demand from an *existing* employer, and what a *hiring* employer may not use as a reason to reject you.
2. **Practice** — what a recruiter does in 20 minutes with an ATS. Law forbids some rejections; correspondence tests still show they happen.

A long unpaid interval **after you already left a job** is usually **not** tjänstledighet and **not** föräldraledighet unless you were employed and applied. It is a **lucka**. Fill it with a true label + proof.

## Catalogue (every common break)

| Break | Law (SFS) | Right vs employer you *have* | Money | How companies treat it when you *apply* |
|-------|-----------|------------------------------|-------|------------------------------------------|
| **Föräldraledighet** | Föräldraledighetslag (1995:584); SFB 12 kap. | Yes. Full leave until child is 18 months; then with föräldrapenning. Return protection. Missgynnande of **employees and applicants** is forbidden (16 §). | Föräldrapenning: **480 days**/child (390 at ~80% of SGI, cap 2026 ~1 259 kr/day; 90 at 180 kr/day). 90 days reserved per parent. | **Most normal Swedish story.** Write it. Do not hide. Law ≠ practice: Ahmed et al. 2025 field experiment — signalling future parental leave **halved** interview invites (low-skill ads). Still: name it; do not over-explain VAB risk. |
| **Pregnancy / seeking work** | DL + FLL | N/A if not employed. Employer must not reject *because* of pregnancy/leave. You need not disclose pregnancy in recruitment. | SGI rules if you later take days. | Illegal to screen out; still happens quietly. Do not volunteer future leave in a cold letter. |
| **VAB** (tillfällig föräldrapenning) | SFB 13 kap.; FLL missgynnandeförbud | Paid days when child ill. Same missgynnande ban. | FK. | Expected. Do not mention as a “risk” in a letter. |
| **Sabbatsår / general tjänstledighet** (travel, rest, “something else”) | **No statute.** | Employer **may say no**. Only if they grant it, write dates and return. Kollektivavtal may add. | Unpaid. No semester/pension accrual on unpaid leave. | “Trendigt” (Ingenjören/Randstad 2023) **and** a nervous HM can still pick the no-gap peer. Fill with *what you built*, not “I needed a year off.” |
| **Studies** | Lag (1974:981) studieledighet | Yes if employed 6 months (or 12 in last 2 years). Employer may **postpone** >1 week by up to 6 months (union consent for longer). | CSN / unpaid. | Easy lucka. Course name + stack. |
| **Start a company** | Lag (1997:1293) ledighet för näringsverksamhet | Yes, **max 6 months**, once per employer; not competing; not väsentlig olägenhet. Notify 3 months ahead. Qualifying time same as studies. | Your company. | Fine if you shipped something. “Tried a startup, no customers” is weaker than a public repo + users. |
| **Närståendevård** | Lag (1988:1465); SFB 47 kap. | Yes while entitled to närståendepenning. | FK. | Treated like family care. Name it briefly. |
| **Sickness** | Sjuklönelagen + SFB sjukpenning | Employed: sjuklön then FK. | SGI-based. | Health is private. “Sjukskrivning, now fit” if asked. No diagnosis in a letter. |
| **Redundancy / uppsägning arbetsbrist** | LAS (1982:80) sakliga skäl | Notice by tenure (1–6 months). | A-kassa if member + villkor; TRR/omställning if kollektivavtal. | Ordinary. Do not apologize. |
| **Quit (egen uppsägning)** | LAS | You may quit. | A-kassa: typically **45 days avstängning** + karens (you caused the unemployment). | Recruiter asks *why*. One true sentence; do not insult the last job. |
| **Unemployment / job search** | ALF / a-kasseregler (inkomstvillkor tightened Oct 2025) | N/A | **A-kassa is insurance**, not socialbidrag. Member 12 months for income-related; inkomstvillkor (Unionen 2026: ≥120 k in ramtid and ≥11 k/mo in ≥4 months). Karens (Unionen: 2 days; some guides still say 6 — **ask your kassa**). Max ~300 days then AF programme. | Completely normal. Using it is not a stain. |
| **Försörjningsstöd** (ekonomiskt bistånd) | Socialtjänstlagen 4 kap. | N/A | Last-resort municipal aid. Must exhaust other benefits, savings; AF registration; from 1 Jul 2026 **aktivitetskrav** in many kommuner. | Different from a-kassa. Not a CV topic unless asked. |
| **Independent building (already left the job)** | No leave statute | You are not “on leave.” You are between jobs or self-employed. | Savings, contract, or nothing. Company + a-kassa: you usually **cannot** take a-kassa while the firm is your main occupation (Unionen: no stacking). | **This is the one that needs proof.** Recruiter cannot see your belief. They can see GitHub, a shipped binary, users. |

Shorter than 3 months: rarely a CV paragraph. Longer than ~6 months: 2–3 honest sentences (Next Bemanning 2026 practice).

## What the law does *not* do

- It does **not** force a new employer to prefer a parent or a self-funded builder.
- Diskrimineringslagen (2008:567) lists sex, ethnicity, religion, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, **age** — not “took a long unpaid gap.”
- Föräldraledighetslagen **does** protect **applicants** against missgynnande *tied to parental leave*. A correspondence test still found a large interview penalty when leave was *signalled*.
- A functional CV (skills-first) is allowed in Sweden (GU job-seeker guide) if chronology is messy. Chronological + one labelled row is more common for seniors.

## Money systems (so the story stays accurate)

| System | What it is | What it is not |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| **A-kassa** | Unemployment **insurance** you paid. Pension-earning. Independent of household wealth. | Not “state help” in the socialbidrag sense. Sveriges a-kassor: *do not* treat it as equivalent to försörjningsstöd. |
| **Föräldrapenning** | Social insurance via SGI. | Not a career confession. |
| **Försörjningsstöd** | Last-resort municipal aid. Means-tested. | Last. Must usually try a-kassa first. |
| **TRR / omställning** | Collective-agreement support after redundancy. | Only if the workplace was covered. |

**SGI trap:** a long unpaid gap with no FK days can **erode SGI**. That is a household/FK issue, not a recruiter talking point. Handle with Försäkringskassan, not in the cover letter.

## How a Swedish SWE recruiter actually scores a long break (2026)

They do not score virtue. They score:

1. Can this person ship **our** stack in 90 days?
2. Is there **public technical signal in the last 60–90 days**?
3. Will they vanish into another year of “building”?
4. Age/parental-leave bias (illegal; still present).

**Proof beats narrative.** Shipped product, GitHub cadence, a live app used for real work > “I stayed current with AI.” One product they can click beats a long repository catalogue.
