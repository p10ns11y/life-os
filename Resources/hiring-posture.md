# Hiring posture

Operator preference. **Not** on the GitHub profile. Use when a thread is **warm** — never as a cold-open manifesto.

## Verify, don’t theater

Some products are not complex enough to justify a six-step gauntlet. Prefer:

1. **One effective challenge** — talk to the strongest engineer who can judge engineering mindset, **or**
2. **One–two week paid trial** — they see the work; both sides save calendar.

Not oversell. Precedent: we have hired on seniority + public GitHub. Many listings are a **checklist to show the company exists**. Walk them when the work is real; skip the extra loops when both sides already have signal.

## Pay

Quoting a number from **public salary statistics** is normal. A company that flinches and hunts cheaper is using a weak strategy: Sweden already has **provanställning** (they can end it before it becomes permanent). Hesitating on a market quote is a **filter**, not a reason to shrink the ask.

## AI vs the Swedish funnel (2026)

Sweden the country still runs. The SWE **process** is noisier: more AI-written applications, slower replies, fewer no-experience openings in exposed occupations, employers asking for specialists who can ship with AI. Country-level employment is not gone. Evidence and sources: [[ai-hiring-sweden]]. Apply as a specialist; do not sit out waiting for a recovery.
