# AI and Swedish hiring (Aug 2026)

**Claim:** Sweden still functions as usual. The disruption is the **SWE hiring funnel** (who gets hired, how loud the ATS is) — not “jobs vanished.”

Do not wait for an AI recovery. Apply as a specialist. Named threads stay on `private/career/`.

## Country still runs

- Arbetsförmedlingen (spring 2026): recovery continues into 2026–27; registered unemployment on a slow path down; **~51%** of employers who tried to hire still report skill-mismatch. That is ordinary mismatch, not a wipeout.
- Hiring **volume drops every July and December** (JobTech / AI-Econ Lab). Late-August silence is calendar, not collapse.
- Shops, banks, unions, healthcare, industry hiring continue.

## What *is* disrupted

| Layer | Figure | Read-as |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Firm AI use | ~35% of firms with 10+ staff in 2025 (was ~10% in 2023; EU ~15–20%). Large firms ~72%. Eurostat / SCB. | Fast **adoption**. Most work is still non-AI. |
| Job ads (Sweden) | Live 27 Aug 2026: **1.21%** of Platsbanken ads *name* an AI skill; **0.63%** *require* it. 2025: 1.06% / 0.55%. AI-Econ Lab / JobTech. | ~99% of ads are not “AI jobs.” |
| Exposure | **39%** of Swedish jobs sit in the most AI-exposed occupational quarter (2nd of 36 countries). | Overlap with tasks, **not** replacement. |
| Who gets hired | Lodefalk et al.: total employment **not** cut. By H1 2025, ages 22–25 in the most exposed occupations **−5.5%** vs less-exposed roles *at the same employer*. Ages 31–49: flat. Adjustment is **less hiring**, not mass firing. | Juniors in SWE/admin/CS are the squeeze. Mid-career specialists are not. |
| Entry-level ads | −5.3 pp fewer no-experience openings in the most exposed occupations vs least (2025). Mixed with the rate-hike cycle. | Fewer first-seat ads. |
| ICT sector | AF: information/kommunikation among the **largest employment drops in Q1 2026**, after 2024 cuts. Same employers still among the **most optimistic** for H2 2026. IMF: ICT high-exposure / low-complementarity; ~1/3 of ICT graduates work outside the field. | Consultant/SaaS overshoot + AI. Not “Sweden stopped hiring engineers.” |
| Skill shortage | Manpower Talent Shortage 2026: AI skill now **harder to hire** than classic IT. TechSverige still ~18k IT specialists/year. | Scarcity of people who ship *with* AI. |

Software/systems developers hold the most AI-skill ads (756 in 2025). Split among those ads: ~46% *build*, 26% *integrate*, 4% *use* (AI-Econ Lab).

## Process (the felt disruption)

1. **Volume, not vacancy count** — AI-written applications flood ATS. Recruiters screen harder or ghost.
2. **Junior tasks cheaper** — CRUD and first-pass screening. Teams keep seniors who own product, infra, review.
3. **“AI” as a JD filter** — most of those ads still want a developer who *builds or wires* models, not a chatbot user.
4. **More rounds, slower calendars** — tests, take-homes, agent-workflow demos. August + election year add lag.
5. **Mismatch** — specialist seats unfilled while long-term unemployed sit.

Sweden “functioning as usual” and “SWE hiring feels broken” can both be true.

## How to apply (operator)

- Frame: **domain + systems + agents/infra**, not junior CRUD.
- Live-check on Jobanni (direct ATS), then submit. Outcomes on `private/career/`.
