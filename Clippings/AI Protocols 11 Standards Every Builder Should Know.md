A practical guide to 11 AI protocols and standards powering agent tools, communication, authorization, observability, and structured data

**TL;DR:** AI protocols define how agents connect to tools, users, applications, APIs, and other agents. AI-native standards such as MCP, A2A, and AG-UI handle agent-specific communication, while OpenAPI, OAuth, JSON Schema, OpenTelemetry, LSP, and JSON-RPC provide the underlying infrastructure.
