# Founders whose firms payroll many people

Culture note under [[Projects/swedish-assimilation/README|swedish-assimilation]] (area [[Learning]]). **Not** a cover-letter essay.

**Point:** High wealth and large **headcount** already exist in Sweden and globally. **lagom** (just-enough) is a cultural preference, not a ban on building a firm that employs thousands. The operator is a **Swedish citizen** — this is not “foreigners vs Swedes.”

Numbers move with filings. Treat as order-of-magnitude, dated.

## Sweden-rooted (wealth + jobs)

| People / sphere | Firm | Headcount (order of mag.) | Source (dated) |
|-----------------|------|---------------------------|----------------|
| **Stefan Persson** / family | H&M | **~95k** worldwide (end-2025, group) | [Modaes, Apr 2026](https://www.modaes.com/global/markets/fewer-jobs-at-fashion-giants-11000-fewer-workers-in-one-year) · Forbes-class wealth lists 2026 |
| **Ingvar Kamprad** (d.) / Kamprad structures | IKEA | **~215k–217k** (2026 snapshots) | [PitchBook ~215k](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/65727-37) · [Revelio ~217k Mar 2026](https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/ikea-group/employees/) |
| **Daniel Ek**, **Martin Lorentzon** | Spotify | **~7.3k FTE** (Q1 2026 company letter); Sweden office still a large SWE site | [Spotify shareholder letter / SQ Mag](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/how-many-people-work-at-spotify/) |
| **Rausing** family | Tetra Laval / packaging | Global industrial payroll (packaging + related) | Forbes 2025–26 Swedish billionaire lists |
| Wallenberg / **Investor** sphere | Atlas Copco, ABB, Ericsson, etc. (holdings) | Industrial tens–hundreds of thousands **across the sphere**, not one CEO lifestyle brand | Public holdings |

H&M and IKEA are **Swedish-origin mass employers**. Spotify is a **Stockholm-founded** tech payroll (thousands, not millions). None of that requires the operator to perform lagom. It also does not require mocking people who prefer it.

Lifestyle press (“lavish”) is not the evidence. **Payroll and products** are.
