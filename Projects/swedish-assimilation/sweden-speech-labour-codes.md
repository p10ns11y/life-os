# Sweden — speech, labour, privacy (codes + links)

Culture / civic reference for [[Projects/swedish-assimilation/README|swedish-assimilation]]. **Not** hiring-manager or Applicant Tracking System copy. **Not legal advice.**

Statutes change. Official text: [riksdagen.se SFS](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/). Readable: [lagen.nu](https://lagen.nu/). Operator opinions on lagom / kollektivavtal: [[operator-public-signal]].

## Constitutional speech (grundlag)

Sweden has **four** fundamental laws. Speech sits mainly here:

| Code | English | What it is | Link |
|------|---------|------------|------|
| **Regeringsformen (1974:152)** 2 kap. 1 § | Instrument of Government | Everyone vis-à-vis the **state**: *yttrandefrihet* (freedom of expression — speech, writing, image) and *informationsfrihet* (freedom to receive information). Limits only by **lag** (ordinary statute) for aims acceptable in a democracy (2 kap. 20–23 §§). | [SFS 1974:152](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/kungorelse-1974152-om-beslutad-ny-regeringsform_sfs-1974-152/) |
| **Tryckfrihetsförordning (1949:105)** (TF) | Freedom of the Press Act | Printed matter: free exchange of opinion, no prior restraint by an authority. 1 kap. 1 §. Also *offentlighetsprincipen* (public access to official documents). | [SFS 1949:105](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/tryckfrihetsforordning-1949105_sfs-1949-105/) · [lagen.nu/1949:105](https://lagen.nu/1949:105) |
| **Yttrandefrihetsgrundlag (1991:1469)** (YGL) | Fundamental Law on Freedom of Expression | Radio, TV, film, some databases/tech recordings — same idea as TF for those media. | [SFS 1991:1469](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/yttrandefrihetsgrundlag-19911469_sfs-1991-1469/) · [lagen.nu/1991:1469](https://lagen.nu/1991:1469) |

Unofficial English of the fundamental laws: Riksdag booklet *[Sveriges grundlagar](https://www.riksdagen.se/globalassets/03.-dokument-och-lagar/bestall-och-ladda-ner/informationsmaterial-och-bocker/sveriges-grundlagar-och-riksdagsordningen-2023.pdf)*.

**Scope note (not a loophole to hide):** ordinary blogs/social posts often sit under **RF 2:1**, not always under TF/YGL (those need the media form / *utgivare* in many cases). The **right to hold and state an opinion** about culture and labour models is still the RF starting point.

## BrB 16 kap. 8 § — *hets mot folkgrupp* (agitation against a population group)

[Brottsbalken (1962:700) 16:8](https://lagen.nu/1962:700#K16P8S1) · [full code](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/brottsbalk-1962700_sfs-1962-700/)

A **spread** message (more than a tiny private circle) that **incites violence**, **threatens**, or **expresses contempt** (*missaktning*) of a **protected group** — or a person **as a member of that group**. Normal grade: prison up to two years; petty: fines; gross: six months–four years (SFS 2024:340 tightened violence + genocide-denial-style clauses).

### Who the statute names (the groups)

The hook must be one of these **protected characteristics** ([16:8](https://lagen.nu/1962:700#K16P8S1)):

| Swedish in the section | English |
|------------------------|---------|
| *ras* | race |
| *hudfärg* | colour / skin colour |
| *nationellt eller etniskt ursprung* | national or **ethnic origin** (courts have treated some “immigrants as such” attacks here) |
| *trosbekännelse* | creed / religion |
| *sexuell läggning* | sexual orientation |
| *könsöverskridande identitet eller uttryck* | transgender identity or expression |

**Not** in 16:8: political party, social class, “people who like lagom,” a company, ATS vendors, “HR.” Sex/gender and **age** are mainly **Diskrimineringslag (2008:567)** (civil), not this crime.

Operator stance on this card: **not against any race or protected group.** Disagreeing with a **norm** (lagom, kollektivavtal) or a **process** is a different sentence.

### Statistics vs contempt (the line)

Public bodies publish tables (SCB, Brå, JobTech, Eurostat). **A number with a source, date, and method is not automatically 16:8.** Spreading **contempt of the people in the row** is the crime-shaped risk.

| Closer to **data** (usually outside 16:8) | Closer to **hets** (inside) |
|------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| “Brå report YYYY, table N: this **category** has this rate. Source: [url]. I do not treat the next person as the average.” | “This **race/ethnicity** is dangerous — be wary of them” as a message about the **people** |
| “Occupation X is statistically dominated by group Y in this SCB cut” + link + year | “Group Y are like that; use the table as a licence to snub individuals” |
| Discussing **policy, institutions, incentives** | Incitement, threat, vermin-language, or *missaktning* of the group *as such* |

A table about a **population** is not a verdict on the **next human** you meet. Using a group average as “caution about that person” is stereotyping. It is also how a stats sentence slides toward *missaktning* of a protected group. Courts look at **wording, spread, and context** — not at whether you “only posted a chart.”

Pulse tags if you cite stats: `source` (agency + table), `time` (year of the cut), `status: tool-verified` only if you actually opened the table; else `inferred`. Allow **not in the evidence**.

Prosecutors and courts decide edges. This card does not. **Diskrimineringslag (2008:567)** ([SFS](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/diskrimineringslag-2008567_sfs-2008-567/)) is a **civil** work/goods ban — related, but not the same as 16:8.

### Why Elon Musk can say more than a person posting from Sweden (not “Sweden is silent”)

This is **jurisdiction + different constitutions**, not a personal exemption and not a ranking of peoples. The operator is a **Swedish citizen**. Musk is speaking mainly as a **US person on a US platform he owns**.

| Layer | United States (Musk’s main legal floor) | Sweden |
|-------|------------------------------------------|--------|
| Default | **First Amendment**: political speech, including ugly speech about groups, is mostly **protected** unless it is a true threat or **incitement to imminent lawless action** (*Brandenburg v. Ohio*, 395 U.S. 444 (1969)). “Hate speech” as such is **not** a US crime. | **RF 2 kap. 1 §** grants expression, but **2 kap. 20–23 §§** allow **lag** (statute) to limit it for democratic aims. **BrB 16:8** is such a limit (*missaktning* / threat / violence-incitement of a **protected group**). |
| Platform | He **owns X**. US §230 / First Amendment practice: the host is not the Swedish prosecutor. | A post **from Sweden** (or aimed at Sweden) can be investigated **here**. X can still be fined under the **EU Digital Services Act** for EU-facing systems — Musk is **not** cost-free in Europe. |
| Stats | Census, FBI tables, think-tanks: citing a **public table** is ordinary political speech. | **Brå and SCB already publish** origin/background cuts (e.g. [Brå homicide 2025:5 EN](https://bra.se/english/publications/archive/2025-07-04-homicide-victims-and-perpetrators), [Brå migration-and-crime 2025:7](https://bra.se/english/publications/archive/2026-06-29-migration-and-crime)). Citing **those** with source/date is not “impossible.” Wrapping them as **contempt of a people** is the 16:8 risk. |
| “Remigration” | US political slogan; deportation/removal is federal statute, debated in the open. | Swedish debate already uses ***återvandring*** / ***återvändande*** (return) and **brottsutvisning** (expulsion after crime) as **policy** — e.g. government tightening 2025–26. Mass ethnic “send the group home” rhetoric aimed at people *as a race/origin* is hotter under 16:8 than “raise return incentives / expel after sentence.” |

**Europe, not only Sweden:** Germany (*Volksverhetzung*), France, UK racial-hatred / communications offences, and [EU Framework Decision 2008/913/JHA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32008F0913) push member states to criminalise racist incitement. The **US is the outlier** among Western systems, not Sweden uniquely “not yet.”

**Musk is not unconstrained:** advertisers, EU DSA, other states’ courts, and US civil suits still exist. Wealth and owning the microphone change **practical** reach, not a Swedish exemption you can copy-paste.

**Already possible in Sweden (evidence):** official tables exist; ministers talk return/expulsion **as law and policy**; researchers publish over-representation with methods. What stays tight is **wording that treats a protected group as an enemy to despise**. That is by design of 16:8, not because Swedes cannot read a Brå PDF.

Do not use this paragraph as ATS copy or as a campaign to mimic Musk’s register. Pulse: `source` = this card + the linked statutes/reports; `status: tool-verified` only for the **links you opened**.

## MBL (1976:580) — Co-Determination Act

[SFS 1976:580](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1976580-om-medbestammande-i-arbetslivet_sfs-1976-580/) · [lagen.nu/1976:580](https://lagen.nu/1976:580)

English: **Employment (Co-Determination in the Workplace) Act**. It is the **machinery** for unions and *kollektivavtal* (collective agreements): the employer’s duty to **inform and negotiate** (*förhandla*) with the union before many important decisions (reorg, hiring practices, outsourcing) **if** a collective agreement covers the workplace.

| Example | What MBL is doing |
|---------|-------------------|
| A company bound by Unionen’s agreement wants to change how overtime is paid | Must negotiate with the union first — not a poll of “lagom feelings” |
| A startup has **no** kollektivavtal | MBL still exists, but the **heavy** co-determination duties mainly bite where an agreement (or union demand) applies |
| You personally dislike kollektivavtal | Lawful **opinion**. The firm may still be bound and may reject you for “won’t work under our agreement.” That is **fit**, not 16:8 |

MBL is not “you must love lagom.” It is **industrial relations procedure**.

## LAS (1982:80) — Employment Protection Act

[SFS 1982:80](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-198280-om-anstallningsskydd_sfs-1982-80/) · [lagen.nu/1982:80](https://lagen.nu/1982:80)

English: **Employment Protection Act**. Default after a qualifying period is *tillsvidare* (permanent / open-ended). The employer needs **sakliga skäl** (objective grounds) to dismiss: typically *arbetsbrist* (redundancy) or personal grounds that hold up.

| Tool | English | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| *provanställning* | probation (often up to 6 months) | They can end it more easily **during** probation. After it converts, LAS bites harder. Quoting market pay is not a crime; they can still say no. |
| *tillsvidare* | permanent / open-ended | The sitting target: not a 5-month consult gap. |
| *visstid* / *tidsbegränsad* | fixed-term | Allowed only in LAS’s listed cases (or agreement). Not a forever loophole. |
| Notice | 1–6 months by tenure (11 §) | Longer employment → longer notice. |

**Example:** You pass tests, they hire on *provanställning*, culture clash in month 3, they end it. That can be lawful **fit**. It is not a licence to publish your private data or run a smear.

## GDPR + BrB 5:1 / 4:7 — data, defamation, harassment

### GDPR (data protection)

**Dataskyddsförordning (EU) 2016/679** + Swedish **Lag (2018:218)** with supplementary provisions. [GDPR text (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679) · IMY is the Swedish authority.

Personal data = anything that identifies a person (name, email, photo, job-application pack, GitHub mapped to a civil name, travel). Need a **lawful basis**, a **purpose**, and **minimisation**.

| Example | Why it matters |
|---------|----------------|
| Putting someone’s application, phone, or thanks-URL on a **public** git repo | Classic GDPR miss — life-os already forbids this (`private/`) |
| Publishing a screenshot of a private chat to “win” an argument | Identifiable data + often no basis |
| Employer storing CVs for recruitment | Usually **legitimate interest** / consent — still purpose-limited |

### BrB 5 kap. 1 § — *förtal* (defamation)

[lagen.nu 5:1](https://lagen.nu/1962:700#K5P1S1)

Spreading an accusation that can **damage someone’s reputation** among others (not just saying it to their face). Truth can be a defence in some forms; reckless dirt often is not. **5 kap. 3 §** is *förolämpning* (insult) — more about the face-to-face / directed slight.

| Example | Box |
|---------|-----|
| Posting “X stole from the company” to a wide feed with no basis | *förtal* risk |
| “I think their interview process is slow and theatrical” about a **company process** | Opinion about process — not 5:1 about a named human’s crime |
| Framing someone as **anti-social** to a workplace or a wide audience to isolate them | Can be defamation / insult **and** ugly politics. Not a “culture” privilege |

### BrB 4 kap. 7 § — *ofredande* (harassment)

[lagen.nu 4:7](https://lagen.nu/1962:700#K4P7S1)

Disturbing someone’s peace: repeated unwanted contact, showing up, messages that molest rather than inform.

| Example | Box |
|---------|-----|
| Dozens of DMs/emails after “stop” | *ofredande* risk |
| One factual job follow-up in hiring hours | Normal |
| Indirect pile-on: others contacted, privacy scraped, “we’ll show who you are” | Harassment + GDPR + possibly 5:1 |

**Workplace code of conduct** is **contract/policy**, not BrB. A firm may reject or dismiss you for **their** rules. Fit-based rejection is **OK**. Invading privacy, indirect attacks, or a smear to paint you anti-social for lawful opinions is **not**.
